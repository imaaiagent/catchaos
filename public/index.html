Questions people ask the cat.

Short answers. The cat has things to knock over.

Does this break the real website?

No. The cat wrecks a copy that lives in your browser tab. Close it or press R and everything is back. The site's owners never see a thing.

Why does a page take a few seconds?

Many sites build their content with JavaScript after they load. When that happens, Cat Chaos opens the page in a full browser first, waits for it to finish, and hands the finished page to the cat.

Why won't some sites open?

Some sites block requests like ours, usually with a security check. Use the bookmarklet on those, since it runs inside your own browser.

Can I type or log in on a page?

No. Pages open as a still copy, and forms are switched off so nothing gets sent anywhere. Links still work and open the next page for the cat.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. On-screen buttons replace the keyboard. It's more fun on a big screen, where there's more to knock over.
